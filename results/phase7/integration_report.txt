3. SECURITY & ROBUSTNESS AUDIT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Path Traversal Defense: UUID-based random directory hashing for all uploads.
- Content Verification: Actual image verification via PIL Image.verify() preventing polyglot attacks.
- Denial of Service Mitigation: Hard 16 MB limit enforced at the WSGI layer.
- Zero Hardcoded Secrets: Flask SECRET_KEY loaded from environment with secure fallback.
- Information Leakage Prevention: Friendly user-facing error templates masking internal stack traces.
