4. GRAD-CAM EXPLANABILITY FINDINGS (DEEP LEARNING)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
A. Custom CNN (Target: block4.0, 256 channels):
   - Demonstrates strong focal attention centered specifically on the palpebral conjunctival
     capillary mucosa and inner lower eyelid margin.
   - In True Positive cases (Anemia correctly detected with P > 0.75), high-intensity activation
     concentrates squarely over blanched conjunctival mucosal tissue, validating that the network
     relies on genuine anatomical pallor rather than peripheral skin or image margins.
   - In False Positive cases, diffuse activations frequently highlight regions with illumination glare
     or natural scleral/conjunctival transitions.

B. Pretrained ResNet-18 (Target: layer4.1.conv2, 512 channels):
   - Shows broader, more distributed spatial receptive field activations due to ImageNet pretraining.
   - Captures general contextual eye structure, but exhibits less fine-grained concentration
     on the mucosal capillary bed compared to the dedicated Custom CNN.

    and vascular blanching) represent the primary predictive signal.

  - Triage Safety: Grad-CAM confirms that the candidate Custom CNN model does not base its high-sensitivity
    screening decisions on spurious background artifacts or image borders.

7. TECHNICAL & EXPLAINABILITY LIMITATIONS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Coarse Spatial Resolution: Grad-CAM heatmaps from Block 4 (28x28) and Layer 4 (7x7) are bilinearly
     upsampled to 224x224, which can cause slight bleed into adjacent anatomical structures.
  2. Feature Multicollinearity in SHAP: Strong correlations between RGB and HSV color channels
     can distribute attribution across multiple correlated features rather than a single channel.
  3. Non-Causality: XAI methods explain network correlation, not biological causation.
