Project Abstract

Research Prototype

Anemia is a major global public health disorder characterized by reduced circulating hemoglobin concentrations, impairing cellular oxygen delivery. Traditional screening necessitates venous blood draws or invasive finger-pricks, presenting logistical hurdles in low-resource and remote settings.

This project, titled "AI-Based Anemia Detection Using Image Processing, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Explainable AI," explores non-invasive multi-modal screening by assessing superficial vascular beds. We systematically developed, audited, and benchmarked 17 machine learning and deep learning pipelines across Eye Conjunctiva, Palmar Pallor, and Nail Bed modalities under rigorous patient-isolated data partitioning, augmented with granular post-hoc explainability (SHAP & LayerCAM).

Development Lifecycle (Phases 1–7)

Structured Methodology
Phase 1: Dataset Inspection & Extraction

Validated ZIP archives (dataset_cleaned.zip and Palm_preprocessed.zip), verified file integrity, inspected folder hierarchies, and mapped physical modalities.

Phase 2: Data Verification, Cleaning & Splitting

Identified exact and near-duplicates, cleaned corrupted files, verified ground-truth annotations, and established patient-clustered 70:15:15 partitions (Train: 5,136; Val: 1,116; Test: 1,117).

Phase 3: Classical Machine Learning

Engineered 151 color (RGB, HSV, LAB, ratios, histograms) and texture (GLCM, LBP) features. Trained and tuned 15 classifier pipelines across Eye, Palm, and Nail beds.

Phase 4: Deep Learning Implementation

Implemented a Custom 4-Block CNN and Transfer Learning ResNet-18 with ImageNet initialization, early stopping, and cosine annealing learning rate schedules.

Phase 5: Comprehensive Independent Evaluation

Conducted unified blind testing across all 17 models on the untouched test partition, generating 95% bootstrap confidence intervals, ROC-AUC, and clinical recall curves.

Phase 6: Explainable AI & Limitation Improvements

Integrated SHAP for classical models and Grad-CAM for deep networks. Solved computational bottlenecks for non-linear SVM and KNN via bounded KernelExplainer; upgraded spatial CAM resolution via LayerCAM and Guided Backpropagation.

Phase 7: Responsive Web Application Interface

Developed a production-grade modular Flask web application providing unified drag-and-drop image inference, real-time XAI rendering, and verified research dashboards.

Academic Research Disclaimer

This application and its underlying machine learning models are designed strictly for academic research and technology demonstration purposes. Non-invasive optical colorimetry and photographic biometric analysis are subject to confounding factors including skin tone variations, ambient illumination differences, hemoglobinopathies, and camera hardware discrepancies. This tool is not intended for clinical diagnostic purposes, treatment planning, or patient management. Standard laboratory complete blood count (CBC) hemoglobin testing administered by certified medical professionals remains required for anemia diagnosis.
