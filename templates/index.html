AI-Based Anemia Detection Using Image Processing, ML, DL, and Explainable AI

An end-to-end non-invasive biometric framework integrating classical machine learning pipelines, deep convolutional neural networks (Custom CNN & ResNet-18), and explainable AI (SHAP & LayerCAM) across multiple anatomical sites.

Supported Anatomical Modalities

👁️ 7 Models Available

Eye Conjunctiva

Palpebral conjunctiva images captured under controlled lighting. Primary modality with both deep learning and classical feature analysis.

Supported Models: ResNet-18, Custom CNN, SVM (RBF), Random Forest, Logistic Regression, KNN, Decision Tree.
✓ Supported XAI: Grad-CAM, LayerCAM, Guided Backprop & SHAP
✋ 5 Models Available

Palmar Pallor

Segmented palm images analyzing dermal vascularization and erythema indices across palmar creases and thenar eminences.

Supported Models: SVM (RBF), Random Forest, Logistic Regression, KNN, Decision Tree.
✓ Preprocessing: 151 hand-crafted color & texture features
💅 5 Models Available

Nail Beds

Fingernail lunula and bed regions evaluated for capillary bed micro-circulation and pallor reflectance.

Supported Models: SVM (RBF), Random Forest, Logistic Regression, KNN, Decision Tree.
✓ Preprocessing: Color space moments (RGB/HSV/LAB) & GLCM

End-to-End System Architecture

Phases 1–6 Integration
1️⃣
Data Verification

Patient-clustered 70:15:15 splits preventing data leakage across 7,369 images.

2️⃣
Feature Extraction

151 statistical color and texture features (GLCM, LBP, erythema indices).

3️⃣
Multi-Model Inference

17 verified models (SVM, RF, LR, KNN, DT, Custom CNN, ResNet-18).

4️⃣
Explainable AI (XAI)

SHAP attributions and 224x224 Guided Grad-CAM / LayerCAM visualizations.

⚖️ Academic Research & Clinical Safety Boundaries

This web platform is built for peer review and engineering verification. Statistical correlations observed in facial or palmar photographs cannot substitute for clinical laboratory testing. Neither this application nor its underlying models make actionable medical recommendations.
