Image Acquisition Protocol

Research Standards
👁️ Eye Conjunctiva:

Gently pull down the lower eyelid to expose the palpebral conjunctiva. Ensure sharp focus on the mucosal surface without excessive direct flash glare.

✋ Palmar Pallor:

Place hand flat against a neutral non-reflective background. Hand should be open, relaxed, and evenly illuminated.

💅 Nail Beds:

Capture unpolished fingernails under standardized daylight spectrum illumination, focusing on the lunula and capillary bed.

Automated Preprocessing Flow

  • Cryptographic Verification: SHA-256 hash checks and mime-type validation.
  • Deep Learning Pipeline: Bilinear interpolation to 224×224 and PyTorch ImageNet tensor normalization.
  • Classical ML Pipeline: Extraction of 151 scalar features (RGB/HSV/LAB histograms, GLCM contrast, LBP texture) followed by z-score standardization.
  • Real-Time Explainability: Dynamic generation of Grad-CAM / LayerCAM / Guided Backprop or SHAP waterfall plots.
