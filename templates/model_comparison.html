Model Selection Criteria (Phase 5 Protocol)

Methodology

In clinical screening applications, raw accuracy is misleading. Because non-anemic cases outnumber anemic cases by up to 15:1 in non-hospital populations, a trivial classifier predicting all negatives could achieve >90% accuracy while missing 100% of anemic individuals.

Therefore, our Phase 5 evaluation protocol prioritized:

  • Balanced Accuracy: Arithmetic mean of sensitivity and specificity, penalizing majority-class bias.
  • Sensitivity / Recall: Ability to detect true anemia cases without false reassurances.
  • Precision-Recall AUC (PR-AUC): Diagnostic reliability under severe class skew.
